Brand platform

Space42 (ADX: SPACE42) is a UAE-based, AI-powered SpaceTech company with global reach, that integrates satellite communications and geospatial intelligence to enlighten the Earth from space.

It operates through two business units. Space Services runs a fleet of geostationary satellites, including the Thuraya mobile network, delivering secure connectivity across land, sea, and air in more than 120 countries. Smart Solutions operates a synthetic aperture radar constellation in low Earth orbit, covering 90% of the world’s population, and uses AI to turn that imagery into actionable insight. Space42 serves governments and enterprises across defense and security, energy, environmental conservation, and autonomous mobility. Major shareholders include G42, Mubadala and International Holding Company (IHC).

What Space42 is — and is not01.4

Space42 is

  • A UAE-based, AI-powered SpaceTech company with global reach, integrating satellite communications and geospatial intelligence.
  • Formed in 2024 by the merger of Bayanat and Yahsat, serving governments, enterprises and communities.
  • Two business units: Space Services and Smart Solutions.
  • Backed by major shareholders G42, Mubadala and International Holding Company (IHC).
  • A provider of sovereign, secure and scalable space-based capabilities.
  • A partner to governments, enterprises and institutions operating at national or global scale.

Space42 is not

  • A space exploration facilitator
  • A collection of independent product brands
  • A satellite operator only
  • A pure software or AI company
  • A consumer technology brand

Clear space

Clear space around the logo is essential for clarity and brand integrity. Use a minimum of 3x units on all sides of the logo; for the icon, use 3x top/bottom and 2x on the sides to form a perfect square.

Minimum sizes

Minimum size guidance for our logo and icon protects legibility across all uses. Sizes vary for screen vs print, and should be followed wherever possible; especially in digital and social placements where different device screens can affect readability.

Accents are never text on white

All three accents sit in the light half of the palette. As type on white they fail WCAG AA, and Sand is barely darker than the page. On white, use accents only as filled shapes — a Solar button with a Cosmos label, a Sand panel, a Pulse data mark. Accent type belongs on Blue42 and Cosmos.

Sand 1.4:1Pulse 2.2:1Solar 2.8:1
!Don't

Accents never take over

Blue42 and white always own the surface. Accents stay within 20% of any composition — one accent per layout, two at most, never all three together, and never as a full background or a block of body text.

A display face that leads. A paragraph face that reads

Typography

Antarctica carries headlines and display. Inter carries every paragraph in digital and print. In PowerPoint and Word, Verdana is the paragraph font because it is installed on every machine. JetBrains Mono is used for labels, captions and figures. Tajawal carries all Arabic copy.
